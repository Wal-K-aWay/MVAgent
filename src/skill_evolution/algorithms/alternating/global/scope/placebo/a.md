### Strategy
Work through the question with the workflow that the prompt already defines.

1. Read the complete question and its options, and note what result must be returned and in what form.
2. Treat the successful reports and completed joint-watch results in History as the only source of visual facts. A failed request, a missing report, or a detail a report does not mention is missing evidence, not a negative observation.
3. Choose the next action according to the Rules and the action contracts above. Copy every video ID exactly as listed, keep each instruction self-contained, and stay within the instruction and clip limits that the contracts state.
4. Before acting, check what the current History already establishes, so that a new request asks for something that is still unknown rather than repeating a request that already succeeded.
5. When the collected evidence is sufficient for the requested result, choose answer. When it is not, choose the available action that obtains the missing part.
6. Keep within the available actions and the step budget; this guidance adds no actions, fields or evidence of its own.
